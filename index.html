Game rules

  1. Each player starts with 5 random word cards.
  2. On your turn, submit a grammatically correct sentence using your cards.
  3. A correct sentence earns 1 point. Explain the choice of am/is/are for 1 bonus point.
  4. Draw a card or discard one to improve your hand.
  5. Example: I am a student. / She is happy. / They are at school.

Teacher note: this prototype validates common Module 1 patterns, not every possible sentence.
